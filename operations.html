01 / New build

Run the centre.

One Dream gets a mobile-friendly workspace for owners, front desk staff and coaches, with shared records across the daily workflows.

Core features

01Admissions and batches
  • Record walk-ins, phone calls, website enquiries, WhatsApp and campaign leads. Assign follow-ups and book trials; record outcomes and convert to enrolment.
  • Choose programme and level, assign coach and batch, track total, occupied and open seats, enforce capacity and manage waitlists, transfers, holds, renewals and exits.
  • Support weekday and weekend schedules for children’s and adult gymnastics, calisthenics, personal training, ADHD-focused development training and fitness or weight-loss programmes.
02Student and guardian profiles
  • Photo, name, date of birth, gender, address, contacts, emergency contacts and guardian relationships; link siblings to the same guardian without merging students.
  • Show enrolment, programme, level, batch, coach, fee and renewal status, history and staff notes. Restrict medical conditions, allergies, injury notes and consent forms to authorised roles.
03Attendance and coach management
  • Mark Present, Absent, Late, Leave or Trial. Filter by student, batch, programme and month; flag repeated absence and low attendance.
  • Track coach attendance, assigned batches, shifts, leave, working hours and full-time or part-time status. Limit coaches to assigned batches and audit approved corrections.
04Fees, invoices and renewals
  • Configure programme, duration and session pricing: monthly, quarterly, half-yearly, yearly, personal training and single session. Set admission fees and GST.
  • Generate invoices, payment links and receipts; track paid, partial, overdue, refunded and waived records. Calculate expiry and renewal dates and handle gateway callbacks and retries without duplicate credits.
05Skills, assessment and progress
  • Basic 1–2, Intermediate 1–2 and Advanced 1–2: categories, prerequisites, coach notes and demonstrations. Initial exercises include jumps, planks, pencil hold, back bend, candle stand hold, handstand, front split, back roll and front/back roll splits.
  • Record Not started, In progress, Practising, Achieved and Needs improvement. Capture assessment dates and feedback; show a skills matrix, progress percentage and coach-led promotion recommendations.
06Private media and consent
  • Upload and tag photos or videos by student, batch, event or skill. Keep private student media separate from approved marketing assets. Enforce parent consent, role visibility and publication approval.
07Owner reports and exports
  • Active students, admissions, occupancy and waitlists; attendance by student, batch, programme and coach; coach workload; collections, dues, balances, renewals and revenue by programme.
  • Report skill progress by level and coach, enquiry-to-trial-to-enrolment conversion, campaign performance and reminder outcomes. Export operational reports as Excel or PDF.
08Infrastructure and optional access
  • Staging and production, database, secure private media, consent records, role permissions, audit logs, backups and restore, and an API layer for integrations.
  • A parent/student portal requires separate agreement. If approved, guardians see only their own student’s attendance, fees, progress, approved media and notices. WhatsApp updates are the baseline.

Role journeys

Owner

Review occupancy, collections, workload and progress; export records and approve access.

Front desk

Capture an enquiry, book a trial, enrol into an available batch and issue an invoice.

Coach

Take attendance, assess skills and prepare progress updates for assigned batches.
