04 / Data and access

Keep the history.

One Dream supplies approved records and retains ownership of its data and accounts. myPAL maps, validates and imports the agreed history.

What to hand over

01Students and guardians
  • IDs, birth dates, programme, level and enrolment status; guardian links, contacts and emergency details. Separate medical, allergy and injury notes into restricted fields.
02Batches and coaches
  • IDs, timetable, capacity, coach assignments, coach type, shifts and available attendance history.
03Admissions and trials
  • Lead source, enquiry date, contact, trial date and outcome, follow-up status and staff owner.
04Finance
  • Plans, invoices, receipts, payment amounts and dates, dues and opening balances, renewal dates, waivers and refunds.
05Skills and media
  • Six levels of checklists, assessment criteria, achievements, dates, coach notes, approved media and media consent.
06Communication consent
  • Contact and channel, consent evidence, preferences and opt-outs.

How cutover works

01Receive
  • Agree the archive period and receive centre exports securely by Week 2. Use agreed opening-record templates when exports are unavailable.
02Map
  • Deduplicate contacts without merging siblings. Map records, flag missing fields and agree import exceptions.
03Rehearse
  • Import to staging and reconcile student counts, batches, dues and historical totals. Test backups and exports.
04Freeze and launch
  • Back up legacy data, freeze edits, import final changes, validate with finance and obtain sign-off before broader rollout. Retain legacy access until migration and archive retention are approved.

Accounts and safeguards

Channels and domain

Week 1: Meta Business and WhatsApp invitations, dedicated number and OTP owner, verification and message approver. Week 2: CMS/form access, subdomain and scoped DNS. Instagram/Facebook permissions only as needed for agreed scope.

Hosting and payments

Week 2: approved hosting, database, private media, backups and monitoring. Start gateway setup at kickoff and test in Week 4. Production credentials follow KYC and testing.

Grexa decision

Decide in Week 1 if Grexa supplies CRM, attendance, payments or marketing records. Confirm exports, APIs or webhooks, sync frequency and which system owns each dataset. Use file import where needed.

Invite named users with minimum privileges and share secrets through a secure vault. Restrict sensitive fields and private media, encrypt stored data and traffic, log access and changes, and give One Dream the ability to export its records.
