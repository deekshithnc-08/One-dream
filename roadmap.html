03 / Implementation plan

Eight weeks.
Two phases.

Every week has a reviewable deliverable. Week 4 and Week 8 sign-offs gate the next milestone.

Phase 1 · Build the centre platform

W01Prototype

Build: Clickable owner, front desk and coach journeys from enquiry to enrolment, attendance, fee status, progress and engagement. Use sample records and simulated payments and messages.

One Dream provides: Brand assets, workflow walkthrough, approver, current forms and representative data. Confirm attendance method and optional portal.

Acceptance: Users complete agreed journeys and approve navigation, fields, statuses and permissions.

W02Data and infrastructure

Build: Set up staging and production foundations, profile and roster imports, roles, audit logs, private media, backups and API layer.

One Dream provides: Source exports, timetable, fee rules, consent records, opening balances and named data owner.

Acceptance: Counts reconcile, siblings stay distinct, and exceptions, mappings and visibility are approved.

W03Start daily operations

Build: Build enquiry, trial, enrolment, capacity, waitlist, batch moves, holds, exits and student and coach attendance.

One Dream provides: Confirm batch capacities, coach assignments and attendance correction rules.

Acceptance: Trial conversion respects capacity; coaches see assigned batches; attendance corrections are auditable.

W04Fees, skills and reporting

Build: Implement financial workflows, six training levels, private media and Excel/PDF reporting.

One Dream provides: Approved fees and tax rules, finance reviewer, sandbox access, curriculum and media consent.

Acceptance: Payment tests credit invoices once; balances and renewals reconcile; coaches produce a parent-ready report. Week 4 sign-off.

Phase 2 · Engage, test and deploy

W05Connect engagement channels

Build: Connect WhatsApp and website capture; configure approved journeys, staff queues and overrides.

One Dream provides: Meta invitations, number and OTP owner, message text, opt-ins, sending hours and escalation contacts.

Acceptance: Enquiries reach staff; messages use correct context; opt-outs and completed actions suppress reminders.

W06Rehearse migration and test

Build: Full staging import, data reconciliation, payment/message retries, duplicates, permissions, exports and restore.

One Dream provides: Named reviewers, corrected exceptions, expected test results and approved master data source.

Acceptance: Enrolment, payments, absence and progress journeys pass. Resolve reconciliation issues; failures create staff tasks.

W07Train staff and approve launch

Build: Train owner, front desk, coaches and parents if included. Close critical issues and document recovery, escalation and cutover.

One Dream provides: Staff attendance, consolidated feedback, sign-off owner, freeze window and fallback approval.

Acceptance: Named staff complete realistic tasks; confirm records, billing, access and message behaviour.

W08Final migration and rollout

Build: Back up legacy records, import the final changes, start with one batch, then expand after checks. Monitor attendance, payments, message failures and support queries.

One Dream provides: Final exports, operational champion, finance validation and launch approval.

Acceptance: Live workflows pass, records agree, and handover includes account access, admin docs, restore instructions, known limits, support and improvement backlog.

Scope decisions

Separate agreement is required for a parent portal, Instagram Auto DM, the One Dream AI assistant, payroll, QR or hardware attendance, and additional branches. Confirm Grexa’s role at kickoff. No FC.one vendor access or live API connection is required.
